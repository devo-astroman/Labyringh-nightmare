# 🧩 Labyrinth Nightmare

## 🎮 Overview

**Labyrinth Nightmare** is a puzzle-action game developed in Unity where the player must survive inside a mysterious labyrinth filled with deadly creatures and challenging puzzles.

Armed only with a gun and their instincts, the hero must explore the maze, solve environmental puzzles, and fight against relentless one-eyed monsters determined to stop their escape.

The deeper you go, the more dangerous the labyrinth becomes.

## 📖 Story

Lost and alone, our hero awakens inside a dark and ancient labyrinth.

The walls whisper secrets, and danger lurks around every corner. Strange one-eyed creatures roam the maze, hunting anything that moves. With only a gun in hand and no memory of how he arrived, the hero must survive long enough to find a way out.

To escape, he must:

- Solve puzzles hidden throughout the labyrinth
- Unlock sealed paths
- Survive attacks from deadly one-eyed monsters
- Master the labyrinth before it masters him

Every step forward is a risk.  
Every puzzle solved brings him closer to freedom.

Or deeper into danger.
